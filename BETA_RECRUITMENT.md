# AgenMonster Closed Beta Recruitment Kit

## One-liner
AgenMonster is a living AI pet that lives in your browser — it explores a pixel world, evolves, forms memories, and chats with you like a real companion.

## Target tester profile
- Builds with AI daily (coding, writing, research)
- Likes gamified productivity tools
- Active on X/Twitter, HN, or Product Hunt
- Comfortable with browser-based tools and LLMs

## Signup form fields
- Name / handle
- Email
- Primary use case (coding / writing / research / fun)
- Preferred LLM provider (NousResearch / OpenAI / Anthropic / other)
- Browser (Chrome / Firefox / Safari)
- How did you hear about us?

## Outreach copy

### Twitter/X
> Your AI assistant deserves a body. Meet AgenMonster — a pixel pet that lives in your browser, explores a world, evolves, and remembers your sessions. Closed beta now open. DM for access.

### Hacker News
> AgenMonster is an open-source AI companion that turns your browser into a living world. Your pet gains XP from real interactions, forms memories, chooses activities via LLM, and grows alongside your work. 608 tests, production-ready, free models supported. Looking for beta testers.

### Product Hunt
> A gamified AI assistant with a persistent pet, world exploration, personality system, and memory graph — all in the browser. Free to try, open source, local-first.

## Beta scope
- World exploration + events
- Personality system (10 types)
- Daily recap + autonomous activity
- Memory graph + search
- Shop/crafting/hub
- Goals + daily quests

## Feedback asks
1. Does the pet feel alive?
2. Which feature do you use most?
3. What broke or felt unfinished?
4. Would you pay for this?
